# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Organization members use the portal to open applications and understand their current access.
Administrators and operators use the administration surfaces to manage governed identities,
application access, directory inputs, and provider configuration. Auditors inspect the same
records without changing them.

## Product Purpose

Access Control gives an organization one authenticated place to understand and administer who
can use each application and how that access reaches external providers. A successful workflow
makes the current state, the source of an entitlement, the effect of a proposed change, and the
resulting audit record easy to verify.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and trustworthy. The interface uses direct language and familiar administrative
controls so that the governed state remains more prominent than the product itself.

## Anti-references

- Consumer account settings that hide identity and revision details behind simplified labels.
- Marketing dashboards that decorate routine administration with oversized metrics or motion.
- Destructive controls that make suspension, retirement, or provider writes look reversible or
  routine.

## Design Principles

- Put the current governed state beside the control that changes it.
- Keep permissions visible: auditors read, operators perform operational changes, and only
  administrators perform identity or organization-level changes.
- Require explicit confirmation for lifecycle and access changes with wider consequences.
- Use stable domain language while translating internal implementation terms for operators.
- Preserve identifiers, revisions, and auditability wherever they help verify a change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The primary interface language is Japanese. All workflows must remain usable by keyboard, expose
programmatic labels and status messages, preserve readable text contrast, and respect reduced
motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status or consequences.
